Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

This policy explains what the Recipe Buddy app ("we", "the app") collects, why, and who else handles it. In short: your recipes stay on your phone, the things you ask the AI to read are sent to our AI providers only to answer that request, and we never sell your data or show ads.

1. What stays on your phone

Your recipes, dish photos, notes, star ratings, favorites, meal plans, shopping lists and settings are stored only on your device. We do not upload them to our servers for storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them. If you turned on your phone's own backup (for example Google backup on Android), that backup may include the app's data under your Google account.

2. Your account

  • Without signing in, the app creates an anonymous account ID so it can count your free uses and unlock Premium. It contains no name or email.
  • If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, name, profile picture link and Google account ID from Google. We use them only to sign you in and keep your Premium subscription with you on a new phone.

Accounts are stored by our hosting provider (servers in the United States).

3. What is sent to AI, and to whom

AI features run on our server, which forwards only the content needed for your request to an AI provider and returns the answer. We do not keep the content after the request finishes. Our server logs only timings and sizes, never the content. We do not use your content to train AI models.

  • Importing a recipe: the photos you take or choose, the text and pictures of PDFs you import, text you paste, the text of web pages you import, and videos you import (with their caption and description) are sent to our AI service providers in Singapore (and the United States as a backup) to read the recipe.
  • Planning and ideas: for meal plans, new dish ideas, AI-written recipes, sorting a shopping list by aisle, step headlines and recipe courses, we send recipe titles, ingredients, steps and your planning choices (number of people, proteins, cuisines, goal) to our AI service providers in Singapore (and the United States as a backup).

4. Usage counts

To apply the free plan's weekly limits, we store how many times your account used each AI feature, and when. These counts are linked to your account ID, not to your content.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions are paid through Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Our subscription service receives your account ID and your purchase history from Google Play so the app knows whether you have Premium.

6. Crash and performance reports

The app sends crash reports, errors and performance timings (app version, device model, operating system version) to our crash-report service so we can fix problems. The app also checks our update service for app updates. These reports do not contain your recipes.

7. Websites and videos you open

When you import from a link, search the web in the app, or import a YouTube or Facebook video, your phone contacts that website (and Google, for web search) directly. Those sites handle your request under their own privacy policies.

8. Permissions

The app asks for the camera and your photos only when you choose to take or pick a photo. It does not use your location or contacts.

9. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not show ads or track you across other apps and websites.
  • We do not share your data except with the service providers described above, which process it only for us, or when the law requires it.

10. Keeping and deleting your data

Account data and usage counts are kept while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time. See Delete your account. Deleting your account removes your account, your usage counts and your purchase record at our subscription service within 30 days. A subscription must be cancelled separately in Google Play.

11. Security

All data sent between the app, our server and our providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

12. Children

Recipe Buddy is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information. Email us and we will answer within 30 days.

14. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
