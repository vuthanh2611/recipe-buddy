Delete your Recipe Buddy account

In the app

Open Recipe Buddy → Settings → Delete account, then confirm.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email recipebuddy.help@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Recipe Buddy account", sent from the Google email you signed in with. We will confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, name, profile picture link and Google account ID, or your anonymous ID).
  • Your AI usage counts.
  • Your purchase record at RevenueCat.

Everything is deleted within 30 days. We keep nothing after that, unless the law requires us to keep it.

What is not deleted by this

  • Recipes, photos, plans and shopping lists are stored only on your phone. Uninstall the app, or clear its storage, to remove them.
  • Your subscription is managed by Google Play. Cancel it in Google Play: Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Otherwise it keeps renewing.
